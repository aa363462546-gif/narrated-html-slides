# Dark Teal Intelligence Template Guide

This document explains how to use Dark Teal Intelligence. `template.html` is the exact DOM, geometry, components, and font-role source; `../layout-registry.json` is the machine source for capacities, slots, asset requirements, visual zones, and canonical fingerprints.

## Character

Dark Teal Intelligence is a restrained editorial intelligence system for research, market analysis, strategy, comparisons, evidence, and metrics. It uses structured report geometry, thin evidence rules, rounded display typography, clear body copy, and large information-bearing shapes.

The template does not require permanent teal. One coordinated deck theme may change semantic colors while preserving this relationship:

- canvas and deep surfaces remain the darkest roles;
- primary text is the strongest reading contrast;
- secondary and muted text remain visibly subordinate but readable;
- primary accent marks evidence and focus;
- secondary accent supports contrast without becoming a dashboard category palette.

Default preset `intelligence-teal`:

| Role | Value |
|---|---|
| background | `#202324` |
| surface | `#292B2C` |
| surface_deep | `#17191A` |
| text_primary | `#F3F4F2` |
| text_muted | `#A4AAA8` |
| accent_primary | `#18E3D0` |
| accent_secondary | `#86EEE6` |

Custom themes must pass registry ranges, role relationships, and contrast rules. Per-page themes and direct CSS are forbidden.

## Fonts

- Rounded Chinese display: **Smiley Sans Local**.
- Symbols, checks, questions, and prominent numbers: **Manrope Local**.
- Body: **IBM Plex Sans SC Local**.
- Technical labels and counters: **IBM Plex Mono**.

The rounded headline and `✓/?` character are mandatory. Fonts cannot be borrowed from A or silently substituted. Publication is blocked until IBM Plex Mono is bundled and all four roles are browser-verified.

## Controlled Variation

- One validated deck-wide theme.
- Approved semantic tones in registry-declared semantic-text slots; a complete title uses at most two distinct colors but may repeat an accent across multiple terms.
- Registry-declared real assets only. Generated images require explicit user authorization in the manifest.

## Visual Prohibitions

- Do not change DOM, coordinates, fonts, type sizes, component counts, geometry, or footer grammar.
- Do not turn ordering numbers into metrics without measured meaning.
- Do not use timeline/arc forms for unordered peers.
- Do not leave required evidence zones empty, fabricate screenshots, or substitute decorative grids for missing evidence.
- Do not add category colors, shadows, gradients, dashboard chrome, or copy mother demonstration prose.
- No video-stage or external-project rules belong in this template guide.
